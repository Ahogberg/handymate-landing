# Handymate Partner Ecosystem

> Working strategy for Handymate Academy, partner economics, certification and partner-delivered services.

## 1. Executive summary

Handymate's partner model should not primarily compete by increasing SaaS commission. The core offer can remain **20% recurring commission for up to 36 months**, while partners get additional earning potential by building their own service business around Handymate.

The intended ecosystem has three layers:

1. **Handymate SaaS** — the product and recurring subscription owned by Handymate.
2. **Handymate Academy** — Handymate's digital onboarding, education and certification environment.
3. **Partner Services** — optional consulting, implementation, optimization and managed services sold and delivered by qualified partners.

This structure gives customers a low-friction path into Handymate, gives partners more revenue potential, and lets Handymate protect SaaS margins while scaling onboarding and customer success.

## 2. Current commercial baseline

Current price plans:

- **5,990 SEK/month**
- **11,990 SEK/month**

Current proposed partner commission:

- **20% recurring commission**
- Paid for up to **36 months** per customer

Partner commission per customer:

| Handymate plan | Partner commission/month | 12 months | Maximum 36 months* |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 5,990 SEK | 1,198 SEK | 14,376 SEK | 43,128 SEK |
| 11,990 SEK | 2,398 SEK | 28,776 SEK | 86,328 SEK |

\*Before churn and other commercial conditions.

The first partner group has indicated an ambition of approximately **20 customers per salesperson in year one**. With three sellers this corresponds to **60 customers in year one**. Handymate's ambition should be to test whether substantially higher productivity is achievable rather than prematurely increasing permanent commission because the partner believes the economics are insufficient before actual sales data exists.

### Principle

Do not optimize permanent commission around hypothetical partner economics. First establish actual data for:

- leads generated
- meetings booked
- demo-to-close conversion
- sales cycle
- customer plan mix
- partner selling cost
- churn/retention
- support required from Handymate

Higher partner economics should preferably be unlocked through **performance and additional value creation**, not simply a higher percentage of SaaS revenue.

---

## 3. Why not make onboarding fees the default?

The recommended starting position is:

**Standard onboarding should be included in the Handymate subscription.**

An obligatory setup/onboarding fee adds cost at exactly the moment when the customer is deciding whether to buy. Handymate should preserve a simple commercial proposition around its monthly plans whenever possible.

This does not mean all implementation work must always be free. The distinction should be:

- standard onboarding/self-service education → included
- unusual migration, custom configuration or substantial consulting → optional paid service

This makes paid services a purchase of additional value rather than an entrance fee.

# Part I — Handymate Academy

## 4. What is Handymate Academy?

**Handymate Academy is Handymate's own digital education, onboarding and certification environment.**

Its primary purpose is not initially to generate course revenue. Academy should reduce the human effort required to onboard and support customers while increasing product adoption and partner quality.

Academy should eventually serve four functions:

1. Customer onboarding
2. Product knowledge/help center
3. Role-based education
4. Partner training and certification

### Strategic objective

Move repeatable onboarding from:

**people → scalable digital product**

without removing access to normal Handymate support.

---

## 5. Academy learning tracks

### Getting Started

Audience: new Handymate customers.

Goal: get the company to first value quickly.

Example modules:

1. Welcome to Handymate
2. Configure your company
3. Add users
4. Add/create the first customer
5. Create the first job
6. Assign and schedule work
7. Complete the first real workflow

A progress experience could show, for example:

> Getting Started — 3/5 complete

The important metric is not course completion for its own sake; it is whether the customer successfully reaches meaningful product milestones.

### Field User

Audience: employees using Handymate in daily operational work.

Short task-oriented lessons focused on what users need in the field.

### Handymate Administrator

Audience: owners, office staff, managers and internal superusers.

Possible modules:

1. Handymate fundamentals
2. Company setup
3. Users and permissions
4. Customers
5. Jobs and workflows
6. Planning
7. Documentation
8. Billing/invoice preparation where applicable
9. Reporting
10. Best practices
11. Final assessment

Successful completion can lead to:

**Certified Handymate Administrator**

### Advanced / Superuser

Audience: experienced administrators.

Focus on optimization, reporting, advanced workflows and new functionality.

## 6. Partner Academy

Partner training should eventually become mandatory for relevant partner roles.

### Certified Handymate Sales Partner

Purpose: certify that a salesperson can represent Handymate correctly.

Topics should include:

- ideal customer profile
- customer problems Handymate solves
- discovery
- qualification
- product positioning
- running a Handymate demo
- pricing and packaging
- common objections
- competitive positioning
- what a partner may and may not promise
- handoff after close

Certification grants the partner/salesperson the right to represent themselves as a **Certified Handymate Sales Partner** according to the partner agreement.

### Certified Handymate Solution Partner

Purpose: certify people who will provide services around Handymate.

Topics can include:

- advanced configuration
- implementation methodology
- customer workflow mapping
- migration principles
- user adoption
- training customers
- troubleshooting and escalation boundaries
- optimization best practices
- managed-service methodology

A Solution Partner can then be permitted to offer approved Partner Services.

### Future recertification

As the product matures, certification can have a validity period and require short update modules for major product changes rather than forcing people through the complete curriculum again.

---

## 7. Academy content format

Do **not** begin by building a video studio or recording a person talking to camera for every lesson.

Recommended content mix:

### 1. Screen recording + voice-over

Approximately 70–80% of early Academy content can use this format.

Typical lesson length: **2–5 minutes**.

The user sees the actual Handymate interface while narration explains the workflow.

Example production script:

- 00:00 — Dashboard
- 00:08 — Open New Job
- 00:15 — Select customer
- 00:28 — Demonstrate assignment
- 00:42 — Demonstrate scheduling
- 01:10 — Save and explain next step

### 2. Written step-by-step guides

Every important video should ideally have a searchable written equivalent containing screenshots or short visual examples.

This lets users solve a specific problem without watching an entire video and allows the same material to function as the Help Center.

### 3. Human presenter

Use selectively where a human creates meaningful value:

- welcome/introduction
- partner sales methodology
- conceptual education
- company philosophy
- advanced training

A real Handymate team member is preferable to overproduced generic content when trust and personality matter.

### Content production principle

Avoid overproduction. Product UI changes frequently, so lessons must be cheap enough to replace.

A reasonable V1 might contain:

- Getting Started: 6 short lessons
- Administrator: 10–15 lessons
- Partner Sales: 8–10 lessons

The first useful Academy can therefore exist with only a few hours of total content split into small, searchable modules.

The economic benefit to Handymate should come from:

- higher product adoption
- lower churn
- faster partner enablement
- more consistent sales quality

Potential paid education later:

- instructor-led training

- advanced workshops
- specialist certification

# Part II — Partner Services

## 9. What are Partner Services?

Partner Services are **optional services delivered by partners around Handymate**.

Handymate should define the framework and quality standards. Partners can deliver the work and retain the service revenue according to the partner agreement.

This creates a second economic engine for the partner without forcing Handymate to permanently increase SaaS commission.

The core proposition becomes:

> Handymate provides the platform, standard onboarding and product support. Qualified partners may provide complementary consulting, implementation, training, optimization and management services.

---

## 10. Service boundary

This distinction must remain clear to customers.

### Handymate is responsible for

- the software product
- platform availability
- bugs
- technical product support
- product documentation
- standard onboarding/Academy
- normal product questions

### Partner is responsible for optional services such as

- doing configuration work for the customer
- customer-specific process design
- consulting
- administration
- advanced training
- workflow optimization
- unusual migration work
- customer-specific integration work where approved

### Simple rule

**Handymate helps the customer use Handymate. The partner can be paid to do work for the customer and optimize how the customer's business uses Handymate.**

Partner Services must never become a mechanism for degrading standard Handymate support in order to force customers into paid partner services.

---

## 11. Proposed service catalogue

These are concepts, not committed roadmap items or final prices.

### Handymate Launch

Optional hands-on implementation for customers that want someone to do more of the setup work.

Possible scope:

- workflow workshop
- configuration
- data preparation/import assistance
- user setup
- launch plan
- team training

Indicative future price: **9,900–19,900 SEK one-time**, depending on scope.

Important: customers should still be able to choose included standard/self-service onboarding.

### Handymate Optimize

A periodic optimization engagement for existing customers.

Possible scope:

- review current usage
- business-process workshop
- identify bottlenecks
- improve workflows/configuration
- adoption review
- recommendations

Indicative future price: **7,500–15,000 SEK per engagement**.

This may be particularly useful 3–6 months after launch.

### Handymate Managed

The most strategically interesting recurring Partner Service.

Concept:

**"We manage and continuously optimize Handymate for you."**

This is not paid access to basic product support. The partner acts more like an external Handymate administrator / digital operations advisor.

Possible activities:

- ongoing configuration
- user and permission administration
- workflow/template changes
- reporting assistance
- training new employees
- regular usage reviews
- adoption of new Handymate features
- first-line how-to assistance
- operational/process optimization
- quarterly or monthly business reviews

Potential packages for testing:

#### Managed Light — ~2,990 SEK/month

- limited monthly assistance
- periodic review
- small configuration changes

#### Managed — ~4,990 SEK/month

- more active administration
- regular optimization
- training/help for customer team
- recurring review

#### Managed Plus — ~7,990+ SEK/month

- larger customers
- more hands-on administration
- partner effectively acts as external Handymate system owner

These should initially be **recommended packaging**, not necessarily centrally mandated pricing. Partner workload, customer size and service scope will determine actual pricing.

## 12. Why Managed Service is particularly attractive

Managed Service creates recurring partner revenue on top of recurring SaaS commission.

Example using the 11,990 SEK/month Handymate plan:

- Handymate subscription: 11,990 SEK/month
- 20% partner commission: 2,398 SEK/month
- Optional partner Managed Service: 4,990 SEK/month
- Total partner-related revenue: **7,388 SEK/month**

The partner can therefore build economics significantly greater than a simple increase from 20% to 25–30% commission, while the additional revenue corresponds to additional work and value delivered by the partner.

This aligns incentives:

- Handymate protects SaaS economics
- partner has upside
- customer pays extra only when receiving extra service

Managed Service should therefore be explored as a **partner business model**, not necessarily built as a Handymate product immediately.

# Part III — Partner economics and incentives

## 14. Commission philosophy

Recommended base:

**20% recurring commission for up to 36 months.**

Avoid increasing permanent base commission before partners demonstrate actual sales performance.

The partner should have three potential economic layers:

1. SaaS commission
2. performance incentives
3. own Partner Services revenue

### Potential future accelerator

Illustrative only:

- Base: 20%
- proven volume: closing bonuses or modest higher rate on new business
- larger proven volume: higher tier / individual commercial agreement

Prefer improvements that apply to **future/new business** rather than retroactively repricing the entire installed base.

Possible milestone bonuses can also be tested instead of permanently increasing commission.

The principle is:

**reward demonstrated distribution, not negotiation before results.**

# Part IV — Recommended sequence

## 16. What to do now

1. Keep the initial **20% / 36-month** commercial baseline while collecting real partner sales data.
2. Do not introduce a mandatory onboarding fee merely to solve partner economics.
3. Explicitly permit appropriate partner-delivered complementary services, subject to quality and contractual boundaries.
4. Design **Handymate Academy V1** around Getting Started, Administrator and Partner Sales.
5. Start producing reusable screen-recording + written-guide content.
6. Establish metrics for the first three partner sellers and their stated ~20 customers/person/year target.
7. Test whether actual productivity can materially exceed 60 total customers/year before redesigning commission.

## 17. What not to build yet

Do not immediately build:

- a large custom LMS
- dozens of polished studio videos
- a complex certification bureaucracy
- every possible Partner Service
- a Handymate-owned managed-service operation
- higher permanent commission tiers without performance data

Validate demand first.
